# 05 · 早餐时刻

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`auto`
- 点缀色：橙红

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide mixed-media illustration of two friends quietly preparing breakfast at a narrow kitchen counter, one slicing an orange and the other pouring steaming tea. Keep the figures in the lower-left two-thirds and let an open window become broad bare paper on the upper right. Use open pressure-varying search lines, repeated only around faces, hands, the knife edge, fruit and teapot. Build clothing and counter forms from broken near-black and deep-indigo masses cut by paper gaps and white dry-brush abrasion. Place pigment-pooling rims, a cauliflower backrun, feathered wet edges and granulation in separated zones. Use orange-red only in the fruit, kettle reflection and several unequal wash flecks. Motif mode auto: omit ornament unless one partial steam-derived curve improves the negative space, then obscure most of it. Simplify all kitchen details. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
extra people, signs, words, logos, elaborate kitchen clutter, photorealism, 3D, clean closed outlines, uniform digital watercolor, repeating decorative marks, evenly distributed detail
```
