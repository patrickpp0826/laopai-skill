# 02 · 窗边黑猫

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`none`
- 点缀色：青蓝

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide mixed-media illustration of a lean black cat balancing on a narrow open windowsill, ears alert and one paw lifted. Keep 35–45% warm off-white paper bare, including irregular gaps inside the cat. Draw with pressure-varying dark-brown and blue-gray search lines; leave many contours open and repeat the eyes, ears, paws and spine with slightly offset strokes. Build the body with broken near-black and deep-indigo masses, split by paper-white gaps and opaque white dry-brush abrasion. Show pigment-pooling rims, a cauliflower backrun, feathered wet edges and granulation in separated zones. Let the cat arc diagonally from lower left to upper right; reduce curtain and rooftops to sparse washes. Use cyan below 8% in several unequal clusters. Motif mode none; no independent ornament. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
text, captions, logo, signature, photorealism, glossy fur, closed cartoon outlines, uniform paper filter, decorative scratches hugging the contour, repeated symbols, floral curls, grids, evenly spaced splatters
```
