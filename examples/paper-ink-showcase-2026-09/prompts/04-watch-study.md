# 04 · 腕表习作

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`sparse`
- 点缀色：橙红

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide still-life study of a compact mechanical wristwatch beside its detached worn leather strap and one loose strap loop. Use a three-quarter top view, place the objects slightly below center, and leave broad bare warm paper above. Repeat the dial, crown, hands and strap joint with open pressure-varying search lines and restrained overshoots. Build the watch and strap from broken near-black and deep-indigo masses cut by irregular paper-white gaps and opaque white dry-brush abrasion. Separate pigment-pooling rims, a cauliflower backrun, feathered wet edges and granulation across different zones. Confine dense marks to the dial, crown, hands and strap joint. Use orange-red below 8% in three small clusters. Motif mode sparse: permit one partial mark derived from the watch-and-strap geometry, crop it at an edge and obscure most of it with wash. No branding or lettering. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
brand marks, text, packaging, human hands, glossy product photography, photorealism, 3D render, perfect vector circles, uniform watercolor filter, unrelated ornament, recurring symbols
```
