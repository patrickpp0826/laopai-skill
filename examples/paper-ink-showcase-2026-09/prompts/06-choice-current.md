# 06 · 逆流的选择

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`none`
- 点缀色：洋红

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide illustration that communicates choosing a direction without text. Show three imperfect folded paper boats separated by widening paper-white channels in a dark indigo current; one small boat turns against the flow toward an open luminous area. Use a high oblique view and sweep the current diagonally from upper left to lower right. Keep at least 40% of the frame as unpainted warm paper. Draw the boats with open pressure-varying search lines and repeated offset edges. Build the current from broken near-black and deep-indigo masses cut by paper-white gaps and white dry-brush abrasion. Show pigment pooling, a cauliflower backrun, feathered wet edges and granulation in separated zones. Use magenta below 8%, only as a tiny inner fold and two dilute blooms. Motif mode none; no independent decorative symbols. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
text, arrows, diagrams, maps, grids, icons, logos, signatures, people, photorealism, 3D, clean vector boats, uniform watercolor texture, recurring symbols, evenly spaced splatters
```
