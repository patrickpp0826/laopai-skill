# 01 · 银发肖像

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`none`
- 点缀色：朱红

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide mixed-media portrait of a calm middle-aged woman with short silver hair and one tiny vermilion earring. Keep 35–45% warm off-white paper bare. Use pressure-varying dark-brown and blue-gray search lines, open outer contours, repeated offset strokes around the eyes, hairline and jaw, and restrained overshoots at major junctions. Build the silhouette from broken near-black and deep-indigo masses cut by paper-white gaps and opaque white dry-brush abrasion. Show pigment pooling, a cauliflower backrun, a feathered wet edge and granulation in separated zones. Place the subject slightly left of center with broad bare paper on the right. Dense marks only at the eyes, mouth, ear and hair turn. Motif mode none; add no independent decorative fragments. Palette: warm paper, near-black, deep indigo, muted blue-gray and vermilion below 8%. Tactile scanned-paper finish. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
text, captions, watermark, signature, logo, frame, photorealism, 3D render, clean closed vector line art, uniform thick outlines, cel shading, glossy skin, uniform digital watercolor texture, recurring motifs, floral curls, grids, evenly spaced splatters
```
