# 03 · 雨中的码头

- 画幅：`4:5`
- motif mode：`auto`
- 点缀色：朱红

## Prompt

```text
Create an original Paper-Gap Ink-Tide mixed-media scene of a lone commuter waiting beneath a cantilevered ferry-pier canopy in light rain, holding a folded umbrella. Preserve broad warm-paper negative space across the harbor. Use open pressure-varying search lines at the figure, railing and canopy junctions, with visible overshoots. Build the canopy and foreground from broken near-black and deep-indigo masses cut by paper-white gaps and opaque white dry-brush abrasion. Put pigment pooling in the wet floor, a cauliflower backrun in the canopy wash, feathered wet edges in the harbor and visible granulation in the distant city. Keep the figure small in the lower third and the canopy as a long diagonal. Use vermilion only on the umbrella handle and two reflected wash fragments. Motif mode auto: omit ornament unless one partial line derived from the railing improves the negative space, then wash-obscure most of it. No signs or words. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Avoid

```text
text, signage, logo, signature, cinematic lens flare, photorealism, glossy rain render, clean architectural vector lines, uniform digital watercolor, repeated decorative symbols, evenly spaced splatters
```
