# 04 · Train Postcard

- Output: `ticket-postcard`
- Ratio: `3:2`
- Mode: `ticket`

```text
Create an original mid-century Japanese illustrated ticket graphic as a decorative postcard about a gentle train journey. Use flat hand-cut colored-paper forms and playful geometric silhouettes with no realistic perspective. Left 58%: a small simplified cream-and-vermilion local train crosses a short bridge over a narrow indigo river, with three simple pine-green trees and one mustard sun; one clear visual center. Right 32%: ordered warm-cream information panel with a large completely blank title space, two narrow empty label spaces and one small newly invented abstract looping-line medallion with no numbers. Use a distinct dividing rule and plenty of paper margin. Warm cream uncoated paper, deep indigo, pine green, vermilion and mustard yellow, selective charcoal outlines, sparse local halftone and slightly misregistered spot inks; bright and clean rather than brown and distressed. The graphic should read like a whimsical printed ticket or vintage postcard at thumbnail size. No readable text, Japanese glyphs, serial numbers, currency, lottery institution, banking logo, real security ornament, postage perforations, postmarks, airmail marks, photographic train, 3D mockup, glossy gradients or copied historical layout.
```
