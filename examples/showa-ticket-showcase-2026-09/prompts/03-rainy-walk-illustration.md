Use an original mid-century Japanese ticket-illustration visual language without a ticket border or information panel. Four spot-ink colors: pine green, deep indigo, mustard yellow and muted vermilion on uncoated cream paper. Bold shapes and clean spatial relationships, no realistic perspective or painterly shading. No text, numbers, emblems, logos, postage perforations, postmarks, photorealism, detailed landscape engraving, modern vector gloss, heavy sepia texture or copied ticket imagery.
